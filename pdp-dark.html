PXENTRA DSR™

Dual-Action Acid Suppression & Prokinetic Care

Pantoprazole Sodium (EC) 40 mg + Domperidone (SR) 30 mg Capsules

PXENTRA DSR™ delivers a sophisticated multi-part release delivery system engineered to treat gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), persistent hyperacidity, and gastric dysmotility. By combining an enteric-coated proton pump inhibitor with a sustained-release dopamine antagonist, it provides rapid 24-hour symptom relief and long-term mucosal healing.

Clinical Rationale

Key Therapeutic Indications & Efficacy

Engineered to resolve complex GI acid-peptic disorders through targeted dual-pathway inhibition.

🧪
Acid Control

GERD & Reflux Esophagitis

Pantoprazole selectively inhibits H+/K+-ATPase proton pumps, suppressing basal and stimulated gastric acid secretion to facilitate esophageal mucosal healing.

Duration of Action 24 Hours Sustained
⚡
Prokinetic Relief

Dysmotility & Nausea Control

Domperidone SR blocks peripheral D2 receptors, increasing gastrointestinal motility and accelerating gastric emptying to prevent nausea, bloating, and regurgitation.

Motility Speed Rapid Emptying

Mucosal Protection

NSAID-Induced Ulceration

Protects gastric lining against drug-induced erosions during long-term anti-inflammatory therapy while delivering high patient tolerability.
