HEAVE™ ENEMA

Glycerine & Sodium Chloride Enema

HEAVE™ Enema is a fast-acting rectal enema formulated to provide immediate and complete lower bowel evacuation. Its balanced combination of Glycerine and Sodium Chloride offers reliable cleansing with predictable onset of action while minimizing systemic absorption. Designed for patient comfort and ease of administration, HEAVE Enema is an effective solution for bowel preparation prior to diagnostic procedures, surgery, and the management of constipation.
