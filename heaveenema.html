About HEAVE ENEMA™

HEAVE ENEMA™ (Glycerine 15% + Sodium Chloride 15%) is a high-efficacy hyperosmotic solution for immediate lower bowel clearance prior to endoscopic procedures or surgery.

Rapid Action

Hyperosmotic gradient triggers evacuation within minutes.

Pre-Procedural

Ideal for sigmoidoscopy and lower intestinal prep.

Minimal Absorption

Targeted rectal activity with minimal systemic risk.

Quality Certified

Sterile fill formulation manufactured under WHO-GMP standards.
