ABOUT PXENTRA DSR™

PXENTRA DSR™ is a multi-part release formulation that coordinates rapid nausea relief with sustained acid suppression. Combining Pantoprazole and Domperidone, it is highly effective in managing GERD, hyperacidity, bloating, and associated gastric ulcers.

TARGETED RELEASE

PROKINETIC BOOST

Domperidone enhances lower esophageal sphincter pressure.

ACID SUPPRESSION

Pantoprazole blocks stomach proton pumps for 24-hour relief.
