THERAPEUTIC PROFILE

Product Overview

🩺

Clinical Indication

Indicated for symptomatic relief of acute and chronic constipation, bowel clearance prior to radiological examination, and post-operative bowel management.

⚙️

Mechanism of Action

Combines fecal lubrication (Liquid Paraffin), osmotic colonic hydration (Milk of Magnesia), and direct mucosal peristaltic stimulation (Sodium Picosulfate).

✨

Therapeutic Benefits

Provides predictable relief within 6 to 12 hours without severe cramping. Formulated in a 100% sugar-free base with high patient compliance.

👥

Target Patients

Adult and elderly patients experiencing chronic constipation, bedridden individuals, post-surgical recovery care, and diabetic patients.

🥄

Administration & Dosing

Standard adult dosage is 10 ml to 15 ml administered orally at bedtime with water, or strictly as directed by a Registered Medical Practitioner. Shake well before use.

MEDICAL PRESCRIBING INFORMATION

Clinical & Safety Data

01 • DOSAGE

Dosage & Mode

10 - 15 ml bedtime orally. Shake bottle thoroughly before each dose.

02 • COMPOSITION

Active Strengths

Liquid Paraffin 1.25ml + Milk of Magnesia 3.75ml + Sodium Picosulfate 3.33mg per 5ml.

03 • CONTRAINDICATIONS

Contraindications

Hypersensitivity, undiagnosed acute abdominal pain, or bowel obstruction.

04 • STORAGE

Storage Conditions

Store below 25°C in a dry place. Protect from direct light. Do not freeze.

05 • SHELF LIFE

Shelf Life

24 Months from manufacturing date under recommended storage.

06 • WARNINGS

Warnings & Precautions

Avoid prolonged continuous use to prevent electrolyte imbalance or habituation.

07 • PREGNANCY

Pregnancy & Lactation

Administer during pregnancy or lactation only if benefits outweigh risks.

08 • PAEDIATRICS

Paediatric Use

Not recommended for children under 12 years unless directed by specialist.
