MECHANISM OF ACTION

How GLIDEPLUS Works

Synergistic triple-action formula targeting three distinct physiological mechanisms for gentle, predictable clearance.

STEP 01 🧪

Lubricates Stool

Liquid Paraffin (1.25 ml)

Coats the intestinal wall and softens hardened fecal mass to facilitate effortless transit without mucosal irritation.

Primary Mechanism Softening
STEP 02 💧

Draws Water Into Bowel

Milk of Magnesia (3.75 ml)

Acts as an osmotic gradient, pulling moisture into the colonic lumen to expand stool volume and soften consistency.

Osmotic Action Hydration
STEP 03 ⚡

Stimulates Natural Movement

Sodium Picosulfate (3.33 mg)

Directly stimulates nerve endings in the colonic mucosa, activating rhythmic peristaltic contractions for predictable timing.

Peristalsis Propulsion
OUTCOME ✨

Comfortable & Complete Relief

Enables gentle, cramping-free bowel evacuation within 6 to 12 hours of bedtime administration.

Therapeutic Result Predictable

GLIDEPLUS™

Liquid Paraffin, Milk of Magnesia & Sodium Picosulfate Suspension

GLIDEPLUS™ Suspension is a clinically proven triple-action laxative designed to provide gentle and effective relief from chronic constipation. Combining Liquid Paraffin, Milk of Magnesia, and Sodium Picosulfate, it works by softening the stool, drawing water into the bowel, and stimulating bowel movement to ensure predictable, comfortable clearance.

Active Composition:
  • 🧪Liquid Paraffin IP - 1.25 ml
  • 🧪Milk of Magnesia IP - 3.75 ml
  • 🧪Sodium Picosulfate BP - 3.3 mg
  • 🧪In a flavored sugar-free base - q.s.
