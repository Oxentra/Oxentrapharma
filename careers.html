Opportunities

Current Open Positions

We are looking for dedicated sales leaders, market specialists, and administrative experts to support our expanding footprint.

Office staff / Office Administration

Oversee day-to-day administrative activities, handle file records, facilitate client communication, and assist back-office support.

Territory Sales Officer (TSO)

Drive product sales targets within specified territories, establish relationships with physicians, healthcare centers, and pharmacies.

Area Sales Manager (ASM)

Supervise Territory Sales Officers, design operational market strategy plans, and manage key distributors in the assigned area.

Regional Sales Manager (RSM)

Lead Regional Sales teams (ASMs/TSOs) to meet quarterly sales volumes. Coordinate distribution chains and execute marketing events.

Zonal Sales Manager (ZSM)

Direct all sales operations across multi-state zones. Manage macro-budgets, lead large management teams, and implement expansion directives.
