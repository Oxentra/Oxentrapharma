OXENTIB MR™

Targeted Pain Relief & Muscle Relaxation.

Etoricoxib & Thiocolchicoside Tablets formulated for fast relief from acute musculoskeletal spasms, low back pain, and inflammatory joint disorders.

Dosage Form Film Coated Tablets

COMPOSITION

Each Film Coated Tablet Contains

KEY BENEFITS

01

Synergistic Pain Control

Etoricoxib selectively inhibits COX-2 enzymes for rapid anti-inflammatory action.

02

Muscle Relaxation

Thiocolchicoside acts on central GABA receptors to relieve painful spasms.

03

Rapid Onset of Action

Provides fast symptomatic relief to restore patient joint mobility.

04

Superior GI Safety

Selective COX-2 inhibition minimizes gastrointestinal ulceration risk.

DOSAGE

One tablet daily after meals, or as directed by Physician.

STORAGE

Store in a cool, dry place.

Protect from direct light & moisture.

PRESCRIPTION

Schedule H Prescription Medicine. To be sold by retail on the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner only.

ABOUT OXENTIB MR™

OXENTIB MR™ combines the peripheral anti-inflammatory power of Etoricoxib with the central muscle relaxant Thiocolchicoside. This formulation treats acute musculoskeletal pain, back spasms, and joint stiffness.

COX-2 INHIBITION

Targeted Etoricoxib blocks inflammatory prostaglandins.

CENTRAL RELAXANT

Thiocolchicoside eliminates hypertonic muscle contractions.

MOBILITY RESTORATION

Restores full range of motion in acute spinal & joint pain.
